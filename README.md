Dungeon Match

"Dungeon Match" is a match-3 card game for two
players created by Arif Nezih SAVI. Using strategy and
luck, players compete to claim a powerful artifact and
become the ultimate dungeon master.
Game Components

• Dungeon Cards: 140 cards divided into Attack,
Potion, Shield, Chest and Cross Cards.

• Two Dice: Used to unlock chests with
powerful abilities.

• Game Board: A 5x5 grid representing the
dungeon.

• Life Point Counters: For tracking each player's
health. You can also use a counter app.

Setup
1. Shuffle the Dungeon Cards to form the
Dungeon Pile.

2. Place 25 cards face-up in a 5x5 grid on the
game board. If there's already a match, draw
a replacement card.

3. Each player starts with 20 life points.

Gameplay
The highest dice roller goes first. Gameplay proceeds
in alternate turns, with each player performing the
following phases:

1- Match Phase
• Swap two adjacent cards to create a match of
3 or more adjacent cards (horizontally or
vertically).

• Remove matched cards immediately; then the
rest of the cards slide towards you, the active
player.

• If no moves are possible, remove the bottom
row (5 cards) on your side.

2- Effect Phase
Apply effects of matched cards. Effects double if a
card has an “x2” icon, but only once per turn,
regardless of the number of “x2” icons. Immediately
refill the board after matching cards one card at a
time, starting from bottom to top, then left to right.
from the active player’s point of view.

• Attack Cards: Deal 3, 5, or 8 damage for
matching 3, 4, or 5 cards, respectively.
Shield Cards on the opponent's side are
removed first before affecting life points.

• Potion Cards: Heal 3, 5, or 8 points for
matching 3, 4, or 5 cards.

You cannot “over heal”.
• Shield Cards: Act as a point of protection
against incoming attacks, placed face-up next
to the player's Life Point Tracker.

• Cross Cards: Grant an extra turn and double
the effect of the next match (damage or
healing). Quadruple effects are possible with
timing.

• Chest Cards: Unlock new, once-per-turn
special abilities.

Special Abilities
Roll a die to determine what ability you get from
matching the Chest Cards.

1. Lightning: Remove any one card from the
board.

2. Mystic Assault: Roll two dice for attack points.
The opponent rolls one die to defend, taking
damage equal to the difference.

3. Phantom Match: Remove three cards of the
same type to create a match. Adjacency is not
required.

4. Vortex: Remove 4 cards in a 2x2 formation.

5. Elemental Fury: Remove all cards of a chosen
type and refill the board. Removed cards are
discarded.

6. Arcane Sweep: Choose a row or column,
remove all 5 cards.

3- End Phase
The turn ends after any applicable special abilities are
used and the board is refilled.

End of the Game

• Win by reducing your opponent’s life points to
zero.

• If the Dungeon Pile is exhausted, the player
with the most health wins. In case of a tie, roll
the dice to determine the winner.
